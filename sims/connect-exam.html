Connect Simulation Exam

You're on a call with Maria Santos, the authorized representative of JJBP Corporation, a new First Circle client. She's new to Connect and will ask for help with a few things. You'll start in JJBP's account while it's still being onboarded, then continue in Maria's admin account once it's verified.

  • You have 30 minutes for everything. The timer starts when you begin.
  • Maria's requests appear in the chat on the right, one at a time.
  • Explore freely. Only the end result is scored, like whether the change was actually saved correctly.
  • Explain what you're doing out loud as you go, like a real client demo.
  • When you're finished with a request, click Done, next request. You can also skip a request, but you'll get 0 points for it.

Skip this request?

Skipping means you'll get 0 points for this part of the simulation, even if you already did some of it. You can't go back to it.

Finish the exam?

Your results will be calculated from what's currently saved in Connect. You can't make changes after this.
