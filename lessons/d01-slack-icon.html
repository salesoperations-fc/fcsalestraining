Set your Slack icon

Add your name, title and a profile photo so teammates know who you are. About 3 minutes. Slack must already be installed and signed in to First Circle (install it first if you have not).

  1. In Slack, click your photo or icon at the top right.
  2. Click Profile, then Edit Profile.
  3. Click Upload Photo and choose a clear, recent photo of your face (work-appropriate, you only).
  4. Click Save Changes.
  5. Check it: your photo shows at the top right of Slack and next to your messages.

Profile photo guidelines

✅ Do

  • Good lighting, face well lit
  • Smart casual or business attire
  • Plain, non-distracting background
  • Face clear, looking at the camera, just you

❌ Don't

  • Blurry photos or a hidden face
  • Illustrations, anime or cartoons
  • Filters or edited photos
  • Objects, food or anything that isn't you
Can't upload a photo? Skip it for now and add it later from the same screen, but do try to finish today.
Also right-click the Slack icon in your Dock, then Options › Keep in Dock, so it's easy to find.
