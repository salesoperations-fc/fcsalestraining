Foundations of Responsible Selling

A guide to becoming a responsible sales person

Ensure trust and integrity in our relationships
Protect the reputation of the company
Promote a positive and productive work environment
Objective: Maintain ethical, respectful, and professional interactions with all clients, colleagues, and the company
As responsible sales people, we should adhere to the code of conduct to:

Read each statement, then choose I agree or I do not agree. You can only submit when you agree to all 16.

1

Avoiding Aggressive Sales Techniques

Remember Aggressiveness in selling can harm client relationships and damage the company’s reputation.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Pressuring a client to close a deal immediately.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Respect the client’s decision-making process and provide them with the necessary information to make an informed choice.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Using scare tactics to convince a client to buy a product.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Highlight the benefits of the product honestly without creating unnecessary fear or urgency.
2

Avoid Making False and/or Malicious Statements

Remember Refrain from making any false, misleading, or malicious statements about clients, colleagues, or third parties. Such behavior undermines trust and can cause unnecessary harm.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: A sales rep exaggerates the features of a product to make a sale.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Always provide accurate and truthful information about the product’s features and benefits.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: A rep spreads rumors about a competitor’s product to gain an advantage
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Focus on the strengths of our products without making unfounded claims about competitors.
3

Avoid Resorting to Physical Injury

Remember Any form of physical injury or threat of physical harm towards clients or colleagues is strictly prohibited. This includes any behavior that could cause physical harm, whether intentional or through reckless conduct.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: A heated argument with a client leads to pushing.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Stay calm and professional, de-escalate the situation, and seek assistance if necessary.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Threatening physical harm to a colleague over a disagreement.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Resolve conflicts through appropriate channels and maintain professional behavior at all times.
4

Using Intimidation or Coercion is Prohibited

Remember Representatives must not engage in any form of intimidation or coercion. This includes actions or words intended to force someone to act against their will or to induce fear or compliance through threats.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Pressuring a client to sign a contract by implying negative consequences if they refuse.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Respect the client’s autonomy and provide clear, unbiased information to help them decide.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Using threats to force a colleague to perform a task.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Communicate requests respectfully and collaborate to achieve goals.
5

Use of Profane Language is Prohibited

Remember The use of profane, obscene, or offensive language is not acceptable in any form of communication with clients or colleagues. All interactions should be conducted with professionalism and respect.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Using curse words during a client call.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Use polite and professional language at all times.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Sending emails with offensive language to a client or colleague.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Review communications for professionalism before sending and avoid any language that could be perceived as offensive.
6

Unprofessional Conduct is Prohibited

Remember All representatives are expected to maintain a high standard of professionalism. This includes being punctual, prepared, and respectful in all interactions with clients and colleagues.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Arriving late to a meeting with a client without prior notice.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Communicate any delays as soon as possible and strive to be punctual for all appointments.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Being unprepared for a sales presentation.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Ensure thorough preparation for all client interactions to demonstrate competence and respect for the client’s time.
7

Adherence to the Law is Required

Remember Representatives must avoid violating local law to achieve sales objectives and at all times.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Getting caught speeding by the police in order to arrive on time for a client meeting.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Communicate any delays with the client as soon as possible and exercise safe driving.
8

Sexual Harassment is Prohibited

Remember Sexual harassment in any form is strictly prohibited. This includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical harassment of a sexual nature.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Making unwelcome sexual advances towards a colleague or client
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Maintain professional boundaries and respect personal space and privacy.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Telling inappropriate jokes or making suggestive comments in the workplace.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Avoid any language or behavior that could be construed as sexual harassment and foster a respectful workplace environment.
9

Discrimination is Prohibited

Remember Discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited. All clients and colleagues must be treated with equality and respect.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Refusing to serve a client based on their ethnic background.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Treat all clients equally and provide the same level of service regardless of personal characteristics.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Making derogatory remarks about someone’s religious beliefs.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Respect all personal beliefs and avoid any comments that could be discriminatory.
10

Corruption and Bribery is Prohibited

Remember Representatives must not engage in any form of corruption or bribery. This includes offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting anything of value to influence the actions of a client or public official.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Offering a bribe to a client to secure a deal.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Compete fairly and ethically, relying on the merits of our products and services.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Accepting gifts in exchange for preferential treatment.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Decline any offers that could be perceived as bribery and report them to management.
11

Breach of Trust is not Tolerated

Remember Representatives must uphold the trust placed in them by clients and the company. Any actions that breach this trust, including but not limited to dishonest or deceitful behavior, are strictly prohibited.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Lying to a client about the status of their application.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Be transparent and honest in all communications with clients.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Using company resources (like Grab trips) for personal uses.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Only use company resources for work-related activities.
12

Falsification of Documents or Information is Prohibited

Remember Falsifying any documents or information provided to clients, applicants, or any related parties is strictly prohibited. Accuracy and honesty in all representations are paramount.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Altering a client’s information without their consent.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Ensure all client information is accurate and obtained with proper consent.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Providing false data on a report to meet targets.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Report accurate and truthful data at all times, even if it means missing targets.
13

Maliciously Obtaining and/or Unauthorized Disclosure of Confidential Information is Prohibited

Remember Representatives must not maliciously obtain or disclose any confidential information without proper authorization. Protecting the confidentiality and privacy of client and applicant information is a fundamental responsibility.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: Accessing client files without proper authorization.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Follow proper protocols for accessing client information and ensure you have the necessary permissions.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: Sharing confidential information with unauthorized parties.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Maintain the confidentiality of all client information and only share it with authorized individuals.
14

Avoid Resorting to Unsolicited Persistence

Remember Clients have the right to refuse any product or service offered to them. Respecting their decision builds trust and shows professionalism.

Scenarios:

  • Incorrect Behaviour 1: A client declines a loan offer but the sales representative forces them to accept.
  • Correct Behaviour 1: Acknowledge their decision respectfully and thank them for considering our services. Offer to assist them with any future needs.
  • Incorrect Behaviour 2: A sales representative keeps on texting a client who has opted out of communication.
  • Correct Behaviour 2: Stop any further attempts to sell those products or services, and focus on other clients.
