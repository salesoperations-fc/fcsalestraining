Asana and Forms

How we get things done with other departments.

Asana

Tasks and requests that involve more than one team.

F
Forms

Company forms connect to Asana. Submit a form and an Asana task is created for you.

Example: reimburse your fitness allowance
  1. Fill in the Employee Reimbursements form.
  2. One Asana task goes to the People Team to approve your gym.
  3. Another goes to Finance to process it in payroll.

One form, two departments.
