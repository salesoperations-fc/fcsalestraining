Mac basics

Shortcuts and tips for new Mac users. On a Mac, ⌘ Command does what Ctrl does on Windows. It's the key next to the space bar.

Keys

⌘ CommandUsed in almost every shortcut
⇧ ShiftHold while typing a letter for one capital
⇪ Caps LockAll capitals until you press it again (green light)
deleteDeletes the letter to the left, like Backspace
ReturnSame as Enter
escCloses a menu or cancels

Shortcuts

⌘+CCopy
⌘+XCut
⌘+VPaste
⌘+ASelect all
⌘+ZUndo
⌘+SpaceSpotlight: search for any app or file
⌘+TabSwitch between open apps (hold ⌘, tap Tab)
⌘+TNew tab in Chrome
⌘+WClose tab or window
⌘+QQuit the app completely
⌘+Shift+4Screenshot of part of the screen (drag to choose)

Trackpad

ClickPress down with one finger
Right-clickClick with two fingers
ScrollSlide two fingers up or down (sliding up moves the page down, like a phone)
Select textClick and hold, then drag
See all windowsSwipe up with three fingers

Tabs, apps and windows

New tabClick the + at the top of Chrome
Close a tabHover over the tab, click its ×
Switch appsClick the app in the Dock, or ⌘+Tab
🔴 Red dotCloses the window (the app may stay open; click its Dock icon)
🟡 Yellow dotHides the window in the Dock
🟢 Green dotFull screen. Move the mouse to the top and click it again to exit
Lost a window? Click the app's icon in the Dock, or swipe up with three fingers.
